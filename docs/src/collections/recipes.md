# Task recipes

If you know what you want to compute, choose a recipe for that task. Each one
begins with the input it needs and follows the computation far enough to
explain the result, keeping the necessary conditions beside the code. Links
to the mathematical lessons and library guides give more context when you
want to understand a construction or explore a different choice.

For setup, [install TamerOp and open a notebook](../start/install.md).
Installation is also always available at the top of the sidebar.

If you are still choosing a workflow, [Using TamerOp](using.md) explores the
available capabilities and their tradeoffs. The [mathematical lessons](mathematics.md)
explain the objects and constructions; [API reference](api.md) gives the arguments,
results and conditions for individual operations. The [topic map](../topic_map.md) brings these treatments
together by subject.
