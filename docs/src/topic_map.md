# Explore by topic

Choose a subject to see the different ways you can explore it. A mathematical
lesson develops the ideas, a library guide helps you work with them in Julia,
and a reference gives the details of an operation. Where available, you will
also find accounts of how the computation works and reports of its measured
performance. An article that connects several subjects appears under each of
them, with links to the same page.

You can use these groups to move between related treatments as a question
arises. For a sequence of lessons that develops the mathematics from an
example or its definitions, use the [reading map](reading_map.md).
